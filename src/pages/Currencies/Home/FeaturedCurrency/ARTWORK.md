Asset type: editorial bonsai artwork for the image half of a premium featured-currency website banner.
Input image 1: edit target, existing thenewbonsai bonsai artwork.
Primary request: preserve the extraordinary juniper bonsai, its sculpted weathered deadwood, green foliage pads, moss and shallow ceramic pot, but replace the pale room with a very dark forest-green studio backdrop (#0b211b) and a dark green-black stone display surface. Dramatic soft directional light from upper right gives the foliage fresh luminous greens, rich natural texture, and warm highlights on the trunk. A faint atmospheric green halo behind the canopy creates depth; all edges of the backdrop should recede into nearly black evergreen. Photorealistic editorial art, calm and striking, no neon or fantasy.
Composition: square image. Entire bonsai including ALL branch tips and entire pot fully visible, centered with 8 percent breathing room around the silhouette; low eye-level viewpoint. Tree fills most of the square, pot near bottom with a little surface visible beneath it. Do not crop tree or pot.
Constraints: preserve the identity and overall shape of the original tree and pot. Change the background, lighting and framing only. No words, letters, currency symbols, coins, badges, logos, humans, watermarks or additional props.
